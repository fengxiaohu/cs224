# Schnabel et al. 2015 — Evaluation Methods for Unsupervised Word Embeddings

## What the paper answers

How frequency, hubness, and evaluation design distort embedding benchmarks; recommends careful task choice and reporting.

## What Phase 1 uses

Informs our evaluation vocabulary (count ≥ 10, exclude specials), zero-vector exclusion, and separate reporting of OOV / unevaluable analogy pairs. Module F placeholder for rank correlation benchmarks.

## Deliberately not reproduced

Full comparison of intrinsic vs extrinsic metrics across many embedding types; hubness correction methods.
