# Levy & Goldberg 2015 — Improving Distributional Similarity with Lessons from Word Embeddings

## What the paper answers

PMI-based matrices + SVD are closely related to word2vec; context distribution smoothing (CDS) and other shifts improve correlation with human similarity judgments.

## What Phase 1 uses

Module A: raw counts + SVD vs PPMI + SVD vs PPMI + CDS (α=0.75) + SVD at k=50. Toy corpus hand-check of PMI/PPMI. Sparse PPMI via scipy.

## Deliberately not reproduced

Full WordSim / SimLex benchmark correlation study; symmetric SVD on dense shifted PPMI at full vocabulary scale.
