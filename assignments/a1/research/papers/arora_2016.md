# Arora, Liang, Ma 2016 — Linear Algebraic Structure of Word Sense (analogy / PMI)

## What the paper answers

A generative model explaining why PMI-like matrices admit low-rank structure and why linear offsets can encode relations—without claiming analogy always works algebraically.

## What Phase 1 uses

Short conceptual note in notebook: PMI ≈ inner product after SVD motivates Module A/B geometry. Module E analogy failures interpreted via polysemy and corpus statistics, not as proof of linear structure.
